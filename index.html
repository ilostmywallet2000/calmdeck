FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. After buying, open your private app link on your iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome) and tap Add to Home Screen. Calmdeck then opens from its own icon, full-screen, and works offline. There's no App Store download and no account. On a computer you can install it from Chrome or Edge, or use the offline file.

Does my data sync between phone and computer?

Not automatically. That's what keeps it private: there's no server holding your data. You can move everything between devices in seconds with Export backup and Import backup in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, and it works with Wi-Fi and mobile data off. If you connect the optional AI coach, a summary of your Calmdeck data goes straight from your device to Anthropic (the company behind Claude) while you chat. It never passes through us. Use “Export backup” in Settings regularly, and to move your data to another device, just import the backup there.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and that's on purpose. Bank linking means handing your login to a third party. With Calmdeck you log what matters in a couple of seconds, and many people find it makes them more mindful of their spending.

What if I clear my browser data?

Clearing site data for the file can erase what Calmdeck stored, the same as with any local app. That's why there's a one-click backup. Keep a recent backup in your cloud drive of choice.

How does the AI coach work, and what does it cost?

It's optional. You create a free account at Anthropic, add a little credit (for example $5), and paste your key into Calmdeck. You then pay Anthropic only for what you use: with the default model, a typical month of chatting costs well under €1. Anthropic doesn't work in every country (for example, not in Russia). Calmdeck Brain's insights are always free and work everywhere without it.

Is the AI coach a financial adviser or lawyer?

No. It's an AI that helps you organise, plan and draft, using your own data. It can explain general concepts, but it won't recommend investments, and anything legal, tax-related or medical should be checked with a professional.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. $19 once. No subscription, no upsells, and all 1.x updates are free.

What if it's not for me?

Message us within 30 days and you'll get a full refund. No questions asked.
