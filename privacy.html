Privacy policy

The short version: Calmdeck, the app, collects nothing. It runs on your device and stores your data only there, in your browser's local storage.

Optional AI coach. If you connect it with your own Anthropic API key, then while you chat, a summary of your Calmdeck data and your recent messages go directly from your device to Anthropic (the company behind Claude) to write the reply. They never pass through us. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that data. If you don't connect the coach, nothing is ever sent.

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. Our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security.

Purchases are processed by lava.top, our checkout provider, which handles your payment details and email under its own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details only to deliver the product, give support and process refunds.
